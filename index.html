GeoSystems Intelligence Lab

GeoSMART@UCF

We are an interdisciplinary research group at the University of Central Florida developing Earth intelligence for natural hazards, resilient infrastructure, and emerging GeoResource applications.

01

Sensing

Observe and monitor surface and subsurface changes using geotechnical, geological, geophysical and remote sensing technologies.

02

Understand

Identify the physical processes and controlling factors of extreme changes.

03

Predict

Combine physics, data, and AI to foresee unseen future.

04

Support decisions

Translate observations and models into actionable resilience information.
